Frequently Asked Questions

Can I save my 3D build and come back later?

Currently, builds are saved locally in your browser storage.

What should I do if the 3D viewer isn't loading?

Ensure your browser supports WebGL and is updated. Try a hard refresh (Ctrl + F5).
